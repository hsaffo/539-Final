About This Project

The Project

Noonie's Picks was created as a final project for SI 539 at the University of Michigan. As a matcha enthusiast, I wanted to build a resource that helps people discover quality matcha cafés in Metro Detroit without the trial-and-error of finding the good spots.

This site features carefully curated cafés with authentic information about their vibe, pricing, and what makes each one special. The interactive filters make it easy to find exactly what you're looking for, whether that's a cozy study spot, a trendy Instagram-worthy café, or a quick grab-and-go option.

Why Matcha?

In 2023 I ditched coffee for Matcha and began to learn about it's rich cultural and incredible health benefits. Finding cafés that do it justice can be challenging, which is why this guide focuses on places that use quality matcha and prepare it properly.

Built With

  • HTML5 for semantic structure
  • CSS3 with mobile-first responsive design
  • JavaScript for interactive filtering
  • Adobe Fonts (New Kansas typeface)
  • Accessibility-first approach with WCAG compliance

Get in Touch

Have a suggestion for a matcha café I should add? Found an error in the information? I'd love to hear from you! This is a living project that I hope to continue updating with new discoveries.
